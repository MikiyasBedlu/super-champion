Put sponsor logos and clips in this folder.

Then, in the organiser panel under Sponsors, give the address as:

  /assets/sponsors/your-file.png
  /assets/sponsors/your-file.mp4

What works here:
  Pictures        .png  .jpg  .webp  .svg
  Motion graphics .gif  .webp (animated) — these play on their own
  Video           .mp4  .webm — played muted, on a loop, and paused when off screen

Keep each file small: under 300 KB for a picture, under 3 MB for a clip.
A wide shape (about 16:9) fills the card best.

A sponsor can also host the file themselves; in that case paste their
https://... address instead, and nothing needs to go in this folder.
